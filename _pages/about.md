Welcome! I am a [Benoziyo fellow](https://centers.weizmann.ac.il/AstroCenter/benoziyo-fellowship) at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/). I received my PhD in Physics from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.huji.ac.il/) and my MSc in Astronomy and Astrophysics from [Paris Observatory](https://observatoiredeparis.psl.eu/en).

I am a theoretical astrophysicist working in both high energy astrophysical phenomena such as TDEs and GRBs and dynamical processes in the center of galaxies.
